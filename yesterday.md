## 2. Critical Infrastructure & ICS Threat Landscape

* **Kinetic and Cyber Interdiction Converge on Russian Internet Infrastructure:** Ukrainian strikes targeting Yandex hyperscale data centers underscore an operational shift toward severing dual-use IT backbones supporting Russian civil governance and military logistics. The sustained strikes on host infrastructure indicate that distributed commercial cloud and ISP nodes within the Russian interior are now treated as primary strategic infrastructure targets.
* **Third-Party AI Agent Visibility Deficit Threatens Enterprise OT/IT Perimeters:** Field findings on autonomous software agents highlight that out of an average of 1,280 third-party enterprise tools deploying embedded AI capabilities, fewer than 25% integrate with enterprise Single Sign-On (SSO) or identity access management (IAM) frameworks. The remaining software agents operate outside centralized identity fabrics, creating severe unauthenticated privilege-delegation paths across corporate and operational technology boundaries.

## 3. Hungarian Cyber & Tech Sector

Crucially, the ministry launched formal administrative audits targeting legacy state IT contracts, specifically probing agreements executed with national telecom/defense integrator **4iG** and the state-funded *Gondosóra* welfare telemetry ecosystem. Additionally, the ministry signaled that the national space exploration program is undergoing critical cost-benefit reviews.
* **Tisza Administration Reorients Foreign & Energy Policy Ahead of Kyiv Summit:** Prime Minister Péter Magyar announced an upcoming bilateral meeting with Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy, highlighting formal state commitments to systematically reduce Hungarian exposure to Russian energy imports and restore constitutional oversight. Concurrently, Foreign Minister Anita Orbán attends the EU Foreign Affairs Council in Luxembourg, representing a coalition of seven member states advocating for accelerated Western Balkan EU integration and unified counter-disinformation strategies.
* **Data Breach at Kifli.hu Exposes Customer Personal Records:** Major domestic e-commerce grocery provider Kifli.hu suffered an unauthorized intrusion into its internal customer support ticketing systems. Threat actors successfully exfiltrated historical user communications and personal data before perimeter access was severed. The Hungarian incident exposes retail consumer identities to follow-on spear-phishing and social-engineering campaigns impersonating corporate logistics channels.

## 4. Global Cybercrime & APT Activity

* **P7 DarkSword iOS Exploit Kit Integrates Crypto-Theft and Interactive C2:** Telemetry disclosed an advanced iteration of the DarkSword iOS exploit kit, tracked as **P7 DarkSword**. The updated toolset features a minimized on-device forensic footprint, automated extraction of local cryptographic keys and cryptocurrency wallets directly from the iOS keychain, and persistent bidirectional command-and-control (C2) communication channels, significantly raising threat risks for targeted enterprise mobile perimeters.
* **Malware Breach at Japanese Contractor Exposes 8.7 Million Records:** Daiichi Kosho, a prominent Japanese entertainment systems conglomerate, revealed that a malware infection at its supply-chain partner Nippon Columbia compromised over 8.7 million sensitive customer and employee records. The breach emphasizes systemic supply-chain exposure whereby Tier-2 vendor compromises bypass the physical and perimeter controls of major technology operators.
