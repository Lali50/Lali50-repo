## 2. Critical Infrastructure & ICS Threat Landscape

* **Unpatched AhsayCBS Vulnerabilities Weaponized for Webshells and Crypto Mining:** Security teams observed active in-the-wild exploitation of two zero-day/unpatched flaws in the enterprise backup management suite AhsayCBS: an improper authentication bypass (CVE-2026-105133) and an operating system command injection vulnerability (CVE-2026-105134). Threat actors are abusing unauthenticated access to drop persistence webshells and deploy XMRig miners masquerading as legitimate Microsoft Edge processes (`msedge.exe`).
* **Insider Cyber-Sabotage Lands Core Infrastructure Engineer in Prison:** A former infrastructure systems engineer was sentenced following a deliberate insider extortion and sabotage campaign against an industrial enterprise. The operative revoked administrative privileges, forced mass credential resets across enterprise directories, and demanded a 20 BTC ransom under threat of wiping primary production clusters, underscoring the acute insider-threat vector confronting OT-adjacent identity fabrics.
* **Country-Code TLD Hijacking Compromises Major Enterprise HTTPS Certificates:** Attackers successfully hijacked country-code top-level domains (ccTLDs) for Ghana (`.gh`), Sierra Leone (`.sl`), and American Samoa (`.as`). Threat actors abused unauthorized DNS registry controls to forge validated HTTPS certificates for high-profile domains—including core Google service hostnames—demonstrating structural fragility in the root-tier Internet naming architecture underpinning global services.
* **GoBalance Tor Flaw Exposes .onion Endpoints to Complete Hijacking:** A cryptographic flaw in GoBalance—a load-balancing and anti-DDoS tool widely deployed across hidden infrastructure—allows external actors to reconstruct private keys governing `.onion` addresses strictly from public metadata. Attackers can take over dark-web endpoints to conduct man-in-the-middle operations or deploy mirrored credential-harvesting portals.
