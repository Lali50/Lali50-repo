## 2. Critical Infrastructure & ICS Threat Landscape

* **[UPDATE] Kinetic Disruption of Russian Cloud Infrastructure Confirmed:** Russian tech and cloud provider Yandex formally acknowledged widespread service disruptions and data loss following Ukrainian long-range drone strikes. The physical strikes inflicted structural damage on three primary data center campuses, forcing the total shutdown of two facilities and severely degrading operations at a third. This confirms that kinetic strikes on distributed IT logistics can successfully induce nationwide cloud and commercial SaaS service outages across the Russian interior.
* **Targeted ICS Sabotage Impacts European Supermarket Cold Chains:** Threat actors executed an operational technology (OT) cyberattack against multiple French commercial supermarket chains, targeting Danfoss refrigeration controllers. Attackers remotely altered thermal setpoints to trigger uncommanded defrost cycles overnight, leading to direct inventory spoilage. Concurrently, European retail conglomerate Ahold Delhaize was forced to isolate internal core networks following a separate enterprise breach, demonstrating coordinated or concurrent threat vector pressure against the continent's commercial food distribution backbones.
* **SonicWall SMA 1000 Pre-Auth SSRF Under Mass In-the-Wild Exploitation:** A maximum-severity vulnerability in SonicWall Secure Mobile Access (SMA) 1000 appliances (CVE-2026-102255, CVSS 10.0) has come under aggressive automated targeting, with over 130 documented exploitation attempts recorded within 48 hours of disclosure. The flaw enables pre-authenticated Server-Side Request Forgery (SSRF), allowing unauthenticated remote adversaries to pivot into internal management planes and bypass perimeter segmentation.
