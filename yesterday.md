While Ivanti issued emergency hotfixes for two flaws in the sequence, a patch for the third component remains pending. Threat actors are utilizing this zero-day chain to establish footholds in corporate and critical infrastructure networks, completely bypassing perimeter firewalls.

* **Joint European Advisory on Connected Energy Assets and Autonomous Agents:** ENISA, alongside the European Distribution System Operators (E.DSO) and the European Energy - ISAC (EE-ISAC), published a joint threat assessment warning of systemic risks in European power distribution networks.
