*   **Critical Authentication Bypass Vulnerability in Canonical LXD and MicroCloud (CVSS 10.0):** Infrastructure administrators faced a maximum-severity architectural vulnerability affecting Canonical LXD and MicroCloud clustering engines, where an unauthenticated network attacker possessing a valid public certificate can impersonate any trusted client without needing the corresponding private key to gain unrestricted control over virtual machine and container host APIs, reported by [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/critical-lxd-and-microcloud-flaw-cvss.html).
*   **Active Exploitation Chaining Across Ivanti Connect Secure Gateways:** CISA added two additional zero-day vulnerabilities in Ivanti Connect Secure (ICS)—led by CVE-2026-111357—to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog after threat actors actively chained them in the wild to bypass perimeter authentication and establish persistent root access across enterprise networks, reported by [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/ivanti-patches-two-more-zero-days-exploited-in-connect-secure-attack-chain/).
*   **Google Disrupts Massive 1.3 Million-Device Android TV Botnet (XCPE):** Google Threat Intelligence Group (GTIG) and international law enforcement partners dismantled the XCPE botnet, which infected over 1.3 million Android TV set-top boxes, telehealth units, and digital signs worldwide via pre-installed backdoors embedded by Chinese manufacturer Videostrong Technology Co. to siphon bandwidth for residential proxy networks and ad fraud, as reported by [CyberScoop](https://cyberscoop.com/google-disrupts-xcpe-botnet-videostrong-android-devices/) and [Krebs on Security](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/11/tech-firms-dismantle-botnet-that-turned-1-3m-tv-boxes-into-proxies/).
*   **Advanced Agentic AI Automation Exploited in Chainalysis Breach:** Blockchain intelligence leader Chainalysis formally disclosed a data breach involving unauthorized access to internal administrative systems, driven by sophisticated threat actors deploying automated, offensive agentic AI frameworks to bypass perimeter security controls, as reported by [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/chainalysis-discloses-data-breach-following-ai-driven-cyberattack/).
