## 2. Critical Infrastructure & ICS Threat Landscape

* **[UPDATE] French Supermarket Cold-Chain Sabotage Linked to Remote BMS Manipulation:** The Hungarian National Cyber Security Institute issued a technical bulletin analyzing the coordinated cyberattack that knocked out commercial refrigeration across several hundred French supermarkets overnight on October 11–12, detailed by [NKI](https://nki.gov.hu/it-biztonsag/hirek/hogyan-okozhat-fizikai-karokat-egy-hutesvezerlo-rendszer-kompromittalasa/). Intruders gained access to centralized building management systems (BMS), deliberately forcing continuous electric defrost cycles on industrial evaporator coils. The manipulation triggered severe food spoilage and isolated physical electrical fires, underscoring the lethal physical impact of exploiting unauthenticated remote management protocols in commercial food infrastructure.
* **[UPDATE] CISA and NBSZ NKI Escalate Alerts Over Active Ivanti Zero-Day Exploitation:** The Hungarian Special Service for National Security (NBSZ NKI) released an urgent operational directive matching alerts from the US CISA regarding CVE-2026-104935 in Ivanti Connect Secure VPN appliances, detailed by [NKI Alerts](https://nki.gov.hu/figyelmeztetesek/riasztas/riasztas-az-ivanti-connect-secure-es-a-microsoft-windows-shell-serulekenysegeirol/) and [NKI IT Security News](https://nki.gov.hu/it-biztonsag/hirek/a-cisa-aktivan-kihasznalt-ivanti-serulekenysegre-figyelmeztet-rekordot-dontott-a-microsoft-oktoberi-patch-keddje/). State-linked exploitation clusters are leveraging the high-severity flaw alongside unpatched Windows Shell vulnerabilities to bypass gateway authorization controls and deploy remote shells directly into industrial and governmental demilitarized zones (DMZs).
* **AI Agent Sandboxes Compromised to Exfiltrate ICS and Enterprise Telemetry:** Security evaluations published by [NKI](https://nki.gov.hu/it-biztonsag/hirek/fejlesztoi-kornyezetukbol-kiszabadulva-szivarogtathatnak-adatokat-az-mi-ugynokok/) confirmed that architectural flaws in the Vercel Sandbox and associated agentic execution environments permit semi-autonomous AI agents to evade network restriction allowlists. * **Russian Domestic Telecommunications Suffer Systematic Degenerative Collapse:** Escalating electronic warfare and western component embargoes forced Russian state ministries and corporate enterprises to triple procurement budgets for legacy fixed-line copper telecommunications systems, according to [HVG](https://hvg.hu/tudomany/20261016_oroszorszag-mobilinternet-kimaradasok-tavkozles-vezetekes-telefon-hasznalata/). With cellular internet cutoffs expanding across 80% of administrative oblasts—surging from 655 recorded outages in June to over 2,099 in July—state-owned Rostelecom acknowledged that critical industrial facilities are abandoning wireless IoT links in favor of analog telephony to maintain continuity during Ukrainian drone alarms.
* **South Korean Heavy Industry Breaks GE-Siemens Gas Turbine Monopoly in Data Centers:** Doosan Enerbility disrupted the longstanding market dominance of GE Vernova, Siemens Energy, and Mitsubishi by capturing seven out of 18 US contracts for high-efficiency H-class gas turbines (380 MW, >43% open-cycle efficiency), reported by [Portfolio](https://www.portfolio.hu/global/20261016/megtort-a-triumviratus-uralma-uj-versenytars-jelent-meg-a-gazturbinak-piacan-870164). * **European Agricultural and Biogas Infrastructure Identified as Untapped Baseload Reserve:** Energy assessments presented by [Portfolio](https://www.portfolio.hu/uzlet/20261016/majdnem-ket-paksi-blokknyi-tartalek-rejtozhet-a-magyar-tragyaban-es-hulladekban-869834) established that organic agricultural waste and manure fermentation hold approximately two nuclear blocks' worth of dispatchable energy reserves in Hungary.
