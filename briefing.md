## 2. Critical Infrastructure & ICS Threat Landscape

Attackers remotely elevated refrigeration setpoints to 50°C, causing widespread spoilage and physical compressor fires across 150 stores. Claroty's Team82 confirmed that more than 2,700 internet-exposed interfaces lacked authentication boundaries, driven by hardcoded vendor backdoor routines that generate daily static administrator passwords based solely on calendar dates (CVE-2025-41450, CVE-2025-41451, and CVE-2025-41452).
* **[UPDATE] Incomplete Remediation Exposes Ivanti Connect Secure to Unauthenticated Root Exploitation:** Cybersecurity agencies, including CISA and MS-ISAC, issued urgent advisories regarding a three-stage exploit chain (CVE-2026-104935) active in the wild since early October, as reported by [CyberScoop](https://cyberscoop.com/ivanti-connect-secure-zero-day-actively-exploited/) and [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/ivanti-zero-day-chain-gave-attackers-root-access-to-fully-patched-vpn/). Crucially, Ivanti's out-of-band updates patch only two of the three chained flaws, leaving appliances susceptible to evasion because internal Integrity Checker Tools fail to register malicious execution residing in non-monitored memory structures.
* **DOE and Idaho National Laboratory Establish Cyber-Informed Engineering Modules for Industrial Grids:** The US Department of Energy's CESER and INL launched a dedicated educational framework to implement "Cyber-Informed Engineering" (CIE) across energy infrastructure, detailed by [Industrial Cyber](https://industrialcyber.co/news/doe-inl-launch-weekly-series-on-cyber-informed-engineering-to-advance-cybersecurity-in-energy-infrastructure/). ## 5. Recommended Deep Dives

* **Physical Inspection and Cryptographic Verification of Hardware Key Pipelines:** Security teams in the financial and digital asset sectors should immediately establish secondary authentication chains for hardware cryptographic wallets. Audit supply-chain provenance, enforce hardware casing integrity checks, and avoid sourcing security devices from non-verified third-party resellers subject to physical supply chain interdiction.
* **Remediation and Lateral Containment for Perimeter Ivanti Appliances (CVE-2026-104935):** Because standard vendor integrity checks fail to detect in-memory execution by threat actor UTA0576, enterprise defense teams must conduct complete network and physical memory forensics across all Ivanti Connect Secure gateways. Isolate exposed appliances behind strict access controls, apply available vendor mitigations, and actively monitor Active Directory for unauthorized privilege escalation and metadata exfiltration.
* **Hardening of Retail Operational Technology and BMS Interfaces:** Commercial facilities and logistics operators must identify, disconnect, and re-architect external connections to Danfoss AK-SM 800A controllers and related industrial refrigeration equipment. Replace default daily passwords with complex passphrases and enforce segmented operational technology (OT) VLAN isolation without public internet routability.
